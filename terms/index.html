15. Note Encryption Disclaimer

Note encryption is a Fables+ premium feature. When you enable it, your notes and journals are encrypted at rest using AES-256, with a key derived from a passphrase that you choose. The key is held in your device's secure storage; only a salt and a verification token (not your passphrase or key) are synced so you can unlock on another device.

Only you can decrypt your notes. We cannot recover them. Because the encryption is end-to-end, we do not have access to your passphrase or your encrypted content, even if you ask us. If you forget or lose your passphrase, your encrypted notes are permanently unrecoverable. There is no password reset, recovery code, or backdoor.

If you cancel Fables+ after enabling encryption, your existing encrypted notes remain accessible and you can still decrypt them with your passphrase, but you cannot set a new passphrase without an active subscription. Changing the passphrase is not supported while encrypted data exists.

Critical risk. If you cancel your subscription after enabling encryption and then lose or forget your passphrase, your encrypted notes are permanently and irreversibly lost. There is no recovery mechanism of any kind, and we cannot decrypt your data even if you ask us. Passphrase loss equals data loss. Please keep your passphrase safe and backed up.
